Why is "👍🏽".length 4?

You see one thumbs-up, and JavaScript says its length is 4. What's inside it? Guess how many characters it's made of.

JavaScript stores text in boxes of 16 bits, and .length counts the boxes. A box holds numbers up to 65,535. The letter a is number 97: one box. 😀 is number 128,512: it doesn't fit, so it's split into two boxes.

But one emoji can hold several characters. A skin tone is a second character after the thumb. This family is four people glued together by an invisible character, the zero-width joiner (ZWJ): one picture, eleven boxes.

So .length counts boxes, not what you see, and anything that cuts text by length can cut a character in half: . To count what a person sees, use Intl.Segmenter.
